# CASPer Practice Test

🌐 **Website**: https://casperpracticetest.org

## Overview

CASPer Practice Test is a comprehensive free platform designed to help applicants prepare for the CASPer (Computer-Based Assessment for Sampling Personal Characteristics) exam. The platform provides unlimited practice questions, full-length test simulations, and AI-powered feedback to help you achieve 4th quartile results.

## Key Features

### Practice Resources
- **56+ CASPer Scenarios** - Extensive question bank covering ethics, teamwork, equity, and professionalism
- **Full-Length Practice Tests** - Realistic 12-section simulations with timed video and written responses
- **Video & Written Response Practice** - Record, review, and retry with realistic timers for both formats
- **Unlimited Practice** - No limits on practice attempts or access

### AI-Powered Coaching
- **Instant AI Scoring** - Get structured feedback on communication, empathy, and problem-solving
- **Competency Benchmarks** - Compare your performance against 4th quartile exemplars
- **Guided Response Frameworks** - Learn how to organize empathy, insight, and action steps
- **Timed Performance Analytics** - Track your improvement across all CASPer competencies

### Comprehensive Coverage
- **Ethics and Professionalism** - Strengthen judgment and accountability with realistic scenarios
- **Communication and Empathy** - Understand how raters evaluate active listening and humility
- **Teamwork and Conflict Resolution** - Practice collaborative problem-solving under pressure
- **Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion** - Demonstrate cultural competence with fairness-focused cases

### Additional Features
- **Bilingual Support** - Practice in both English and French
- **Progress Dashboard** - Monitor streaks, improvement areas, and practice history
- **Typing Speed Test** - Prepare for timed written responses
- **Discord Community** - Connect with other applicants for support
